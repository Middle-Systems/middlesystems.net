Secy

Software supply-chain security that connects what you declared, what you built, and what's actually running, then tells you which risks matter.

Request early access

The problem

Most teams have more vulnerability data than they can use. Scanners flag thousands of CVEs in libraries that may never ship, may not be reachable, or may already be ruled out by a vendor. Meanwhile, the one that's running in production and actively exploited gets lost in the noise.

Secy builds a single, correlated picture across the software lifecycle so a finding goes from "exists in a library somewhere" to "running in prod, not mitigated, and known to be exploited."

  • Prioritized findings, not a raw CVE list
  • Source, build, and deployed inventories linked together
  • Drift detection between lifecycle stages
  • Owners inferred from your repos and cloud tags

What Secy brings together

Secy ingests the data you already produce and correlates it into one model.

Bills of materials

CycloneDX and SPDX SBOMs, VEX statements, and CBOMs for cryptographic inventory.

Cloud inventory

AWS, Azure, Kubernetes, and container registries, so you know what's actually deployed.

Code repositories

GitHub integration to tie components back to the repo, commit, and team that owns them.

Threat intelligence

Vulnerability, EPSS, KEV, OSV, malware, and advisory feeds, each configurable per tenant.

Deployment options

Secy is planned for both hosted SaaS and self-hosted deployment, for teams that need their inventory data to stay inside their own environment.

Secy is currently in active development. If you'd like to be an early design partner or get notified at launch, get in touch.
