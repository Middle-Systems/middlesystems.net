About

The layer between your systems, done right.

MiddleSystems is a Kansas City engineering practice focused on secure integration, middleware, and software supply-chain security.

Why we exist

Every business runs on a patchwork of platforms: contact center, HR, cloud, CI/CD, and the tools in between. The vendors each solve their own piece. The work of making those pieces fit together, securely, usually falls to whoever has time, and often nobody does.

That middle layer is where we work. We design and build the integrations, migrations, and pipelines that make separate systems behave like one, with security treated as part of the design rather than an afterthought.

Experience

A decade of hands-on work across software engineering, architecture, DevOps, and DevSecOps, with a focus on REST APIs, middleware, microservices, and securing the SDLC and software supply chain.

How we work

Honest scoping

Security by default

Least privilege, auditable changes, and supply-chain hygiene are built into everything we ship.

You own the result
